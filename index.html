02. HONG KONG 2026–2036 // 10-YEAR MARITIME ELECTRIFICATION MASTER PLAN

One Unified 800V Hydrofoil Platform for Passenger & Freight Transit

Just as modular electric chassis transformed road transport over the past decade, BAYWING is engineered to electrify 100% of Hong Kong's ferry and coastal logistics network over the next ten years. Built around a standardized carbon-aluminium hull, high-safety LFP battery bays, submerged active hydrofoils, and dockside BESS buffers, the platform scales seamlessly from 25-seat harbour shuttles to 400-passenger island liners and roll-on/roll-off (Ro-Ro) utility freighters.

04A. Phase 0: Software-in-the-Loop (SIL) Validation (Months 1–9 | TRL 3 → 4)

Digital Twin & Algorithmic De-Risking

Before committing capital to physical hull fabrication, we execute a software-first development strategy at HKSTP. Using a 6-DOF hydrodynamic Digital Twin and Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) benchtop, we validate our 100Hz flight-control laws mathematically before water trials.

1. Model-in-the-Loop (MIL)

6-DOF hydrodynamic physics engine in Gazebo/MATLAB simulating hull wave resistance, T-foil lift/drag, cavitation limits, and free-surface proximity.

2. Software-in-the-Loop (SIL)

Real-time C++/RTOS flight control algorithms tested against virtual Victoria Harbour chop and Lantau swell (JONSWAP spectra) to prove 100 Hz stability.

3. Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL)

Flashing validated control code onto target Jetson/STM32 compute nodes connected to waterproof servo actuators ensuring sub-8ms deterministic latency.

04B. Phase 1: Sub-Scale Validation (Months 6–14 | TRL 5 → 6)

BAYWING-D1 (1:8 Scale Autonomous Testbed)

Because pitch and heave oscillation frequencies scale inversely with vessel size, a 3.56-meter Froude-scaled model (Fn = 1.17 at 13.4 knots) is dynamically more demanding to stabilize than a 28.5-meter ferry. Proving 100Hz active foil control on the D1 in Tolo Harbour de-risks the entire commercial fleet.
