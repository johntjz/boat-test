HONG KONG 2026–2036 // THE "EV ON WATER" MASTER PLAN

Transforming Every HK Ferry & Island Cargo Route in 10 Years

Just as electric cars and commercial trucks transformed Hong Kong's roads over the past decade, BAYWING is engineered to electrify 100% of Hong Kong's waterways over the next ten years. Think of our platform as an EV on water: an unsinkable, modular carbon-aluminium "skateboard" hull packed with safe LFP marine batteries, lifted above the waves on computer-controlled hydrofoils, and recharged in 8–15 minutes via floating dockside battery buffers—carrying commuters by day and roll-on/roll-off (Ro-Ro) island freight by night.

01. RADICAL EFFICIENCY
-78% OPEX

Hydrofoils lift the hull out of the water, cutting drag by 80% and slashing energy cost from $22.50/NM to $4.90/NM.

02. RAPID CHARGING
8–15 MIN

Dockside BESS buffers trickle-charge off standard HKE LV street power and deliver megawatt DC top-ups while boarding.

03. PAX + RO-RO CARGO
25–400 PAX

04. ULTRA-SAFE & SMOOTH
