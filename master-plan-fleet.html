HONG KONG & GBA 10-YEAR MASTER PLAN (2026–2036)

Transforming Every HK Ferry & Island Cargo Route in 10 Years

Just as electric cars and commercial trucks transformed Hong Kong's roads over the past decade, BAYWING is engineered to electrify 100% of Hong Kong's waterways over the next ten years. Think of our platform as an EV on water: an unsinkable, modular carbon-aluminium hull packed with safe LFP marine batteries, lifted above the waves on computer-controlled hydrofoils, and recharged in 8–15 minutes via floating dockside battery buffers.

MODULAR PRODUCT ARCHITECTURE

Five Vessel Classes Scaled from One Core Powertrain
