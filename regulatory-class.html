DNV High-Speed Craft Advisory & HKMPB Green Maritime Hub Roadmap

PARTNER 01 // DNV HONG KONG ADVISORY TEAM (FILED OCT 16, 2026)

High-Speed Craft (HSC) Classification & Software Certification

Our Phase 2 commercial pilot (`BAYWING-D2`, HK$ 20.0M total capital including HK$ 1.5M dedicated to testing and HKMD/DNV certification) and Phase 3 flagship (`EF-150`) are engineered around three core DNV marine classification pillars[cite: 1]:

D2 CERTIFICATION BUDGET ALLOCATION HK$ 1,500,000 Part of HK$ 20.0M D2 Pilot Capital Stack
01. POWERTRAIN & BATTERY RULES

800V DC Marine LFP & Dual-Redundant Pods

Designed under DNV Rules for Classification of High-Speed Craft (HSC) and Battery-Powered Vessels. Integrates thermally isolated 800V DC Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) battery bays (400 kWh on D2; 1.2 MWh on EF-150) paired with dual-redundant electric rim-drive / pod propulsors[cite: 1].

Thermal Runaway Mitigation: LFP Cell-Level Isolation
02. FLIGHT CONTROL SOFTWARE IP

100Hz Triple-Redundant Fly-by-Wire RTOS

Deterministic C++/RTOS active surface control loops operating at 100Hz (<8ms actuator response) across dual STM32H7 and NVIDIA Jetson compute nodes[cite: 1]. Features triple-modular sensor fusion and fail-safe hull-borne displacement fallback.

Fail-Safe Mode: Graceful Displacement Settle
03. SHORE-SIDE FAST CHARGING

Dockside MCS & Unidirectional BESS Pontoons

Interfaces 500 kW (D2) up to 3.5–3.8 MW Megawatt Charging System (MCS) DC shore power via floating BESS buffer pontoons[cite: 1]. Hardware-interlocked reverse-power protection ensures 100% unidirectional grid draw from HK Electric and CLP low-voltage feeders.

Grid Export Protection: Zero Backfeed Guaranteed
PARTNER 02 // HONG KONG MARITIME AND PORT BOARD (HKMPB) SECRETARIAT

Sovereign Green Maritime Hub Leadership & Fleet Modernization (HKD)

As communicated to the HKMPB Secretariat on October 16, 2026, BAYWING provides a Hong Kong-engineered technological solution to modernize all passenger ferry routes and Greater Bay Area cross-border corridors over a 10-year master plan[cite: 1]:

01. 78% OPEX REDUCTION (HKD)
HK$ 38.22 / NM

Slashes energy operating costs by 78%—from approximately HK$ 175.50 per nautical mile (legacy marine diesel gas oil) down to HK$ 38.22 per nautical mile (electric hydrofoil)[cite: 1].

02. LOCAL HK IP + GBA MANUFACTURING
HK$ 3.20M R&D

Combines high-margin 100Hz active fly-by-wire flight control software developed in Hong Kong (HK$ 3,198,000 Phase 0–1 R&D budget) with regional GBA advanced manufacturing for global export potential[cite: 1].

03. EAST ASIA GREEN MARITIME HUB
Zero Emissions

Positions Hong Kong as an early adopter of megawatt fast-charging and hydrofoil electrification in East Asia[cite: 1]—eliminating carbon emissions across Victoria Harbour, Outlying Islands, and SkyPier/Macau terminals.
